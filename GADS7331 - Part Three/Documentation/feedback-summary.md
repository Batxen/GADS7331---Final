What did the feedback address:

The feedback given to me by interviewees focused on how the LLM model could be improved for future editions, additional tools I could use to my advantage, the limitations of the LLM implementation, and how the game could be improved visually.

Initial Reactions:

The initial reactions I saw from the interviewees ranged from supportive of the idea to critical of the use of AI LLM within video games. The interviewees who were willing to give me support by taking part in the feedback were the attendees supportive of AI and showed interest and support within the project after I explained what my concept for a LLM integration could look like. However, I also experienced a lot of discomfort from other attendees I attempted to work with when I told them this project is centred around the implementation of AI LLMs into a video game development setting. These attendees were the ones that chose not to take part in the feedback, be it for personal reasons and belief, believing they do not understand enough about AI to give valuable feedback, or were just uncomfortable with the idea of AI LLM within video games. While they did not contribute to the final feedback I do believe it important to note the reactions and reasoning behind those that chose not to take part as is their right to choose for themselves in the interaction.
